Website Plan

Kampala Marriott Hotel Visitor Guide

This is my plan for an informational website about Kampala Marriott Hotel and its location in Uganda.

About my project: I am creating this website as an independent class project. It is not an official Marriott website, and it will not take bookings. I will cite the source of the logo image on my finished website’s references page.

1. Site Name

Kampala Marriott Hotel Visitor Guide

Why I chose this name: I chose this name because it tells visitors what my website is about and where the hotel is located. I added “Visitor Guide” to show that my project will share information for people interested in the hotel and Kampala.

2. Site Purpose

I want my website to help visitors learn about Kampala Marriott Hotel, its location, rooms, dining, amenities, and meeting facilities. I also plan to share information about selected attractions in Kampala so visitors can learn more about places they may want to explore.

I plan to organize the website into three pages. The Home page will introduce the hotel and its location. The Rooms and Dining page will describe publicly listed rooms, restaurants, and amenities. The Meetings and Kampala page will cover event facilities, nearby places, and a contact form for my class project.

I would like to add JavaScript features that let visitors filter information cards and save a favorite item in localStorage. The form will show an on-page confirmation, but it will not send a real hotel inquiry or booking.

I will check hotel details against public sources and cite any outside information or images I use on a references page in my finished website.

3. Scenarios

I chose these questions because they reflect what a visitor might want to know:

  • Where is the hotel in Kampala, and how far is it from Entebbe International Airport?
  • What dining, wellness, and meeting facilities does the hotel list?
  • What cultural attractions can I visit while I am in Kampala?

4. Color Schema

I chose navy, warm cream, and gold because I want the website to feel calm and polished while keeping the text easy to read. This is my own color choice for the class project, not Marriott’s official palette.

Deep navy — #17324D
I will use navy for the header, footer, and main headings. White text will appear on the navy background.

Warm cream — #F6F1E7
I will use cream for the page background and light panels, with dark text for readability.

Warm gold — #8A5B13
I will use gold for small accents, links, and section underlines on light backgrounds.

I have applied these colors to this planning document as well: navy appears in the header and headings, cream in the page background and wireframe panels, and gold in links and accents.

5. Typography

I chose two common font families so the text should remain readable on different devices.

Georgia, serif — headings
I will use Georgia for the page title and section headings because I like its clear, classic style.

Arial, sans-serif — body text
I will use Arial for paragraphs, navigation, lists, form labels, and captions because it is simple to read on screens.

I have applied both font choices to this planning document.
